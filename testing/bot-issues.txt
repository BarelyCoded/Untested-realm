# Bot play issues

Running list of ways the test bot misplays, so results can be read with the right caution and fixes
can be picked in order. Source for each round is noted.

## Round 2 — field screen with the "considered plays" bot (3,300 games, 10 Nov 2026)

Health: 5 crashes/timeouts, 9 rejected actions in 8 games, 71 draws at the 80-turn limit (2.2%),
no turn hit the per-turn action cap. Slowest decision per game: median 53 ms, 95th percentile 293 ms,
worst 5.8 s. First player won 51.3% of decided games.

## Round 1 — fixed

- Non-damage magics never cast (Grapple Shot, Whirling Blades, Swap, Riptide, Geyser, Displace, Blink,
  Common Sense, Landmass, Earthquake, Gift of the Serpent) — fixed by playing casts out on a copy.
- Archimago, Geomancer and Imposter never used their avatar abilities — fixed the same way.
- Endless self-repeating prompt (Evil Twin copying Selfsame Simulacrum) — declined after three repeats.
- Toolbox / Silver Bullet never used — fixed earlier with collection play-outs.
